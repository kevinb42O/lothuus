# Owner administration

MODE: Operate. Primary target: src/app/admin/page.tsx. Related: src/components/admin/admin.tsx, src/components/admin/admin.css.

Purpose: a single owner manages photographs and prices, reservations, manual bank receipts, customer purchase/contact history and local email previews. Inherit the Manrope wordmark, square forms, flat rules and editorial spacing. Operational surfaces use neutral off-white/charcoal with raspberry primary actions, restrained green success states and plum review status, honoring the user's no-brown-surfaces preference. No shadows or decorative cards.

Structure: persistent left navigation on desktop; horizontal navigation on phones; one clear task heading and primary action; searchable ledger tables; split editor and publication/customer actions. Phone tables scroll within their own wrapper. Distinguish reserved, paid and handed over; local mail is always labelled preview.

Verification: Chrome desktop around 1440px and in-app narrow viewport around 311px. Real test records live only in an isolated temporary data directory, never in the owner's default data store.
