# Owner administration

MODE: Operate. Primary target: src/app/admin/page.tsx. Related: src/components/admin/admin.tsx, src/components/admin/admin.css.

Purpose: a single owner manages photographs and prices, reservations, manual bank receipts, customer purchase/contact history and local email previews. Inherit the Manrope wordmark, square forms, flat rules and editorial spacing. Operational surfaces use neutral off-white/charcoal with charcoal primary actions (#262626, hover #111111), neutral gray charts, white content surfaces and restrained semantic status colors, honoring the user's no-brown-surfaces preference. No shadows or decorative cards.

Structure: persistent left navigation on desktop; horizontal navigation on phones; one clear task heading and primary action; searchable ledger tables; split editor and publication/customer actions. Phone tables scroll within their own wrapper. Distinguish reserved, paid and handed over; local mail is always labelled preview.

Verification: Chrome desktop around 1440px and in-app narrow viewport around 311px. Real test records live only in an isolated temporary data directory, never in the owner's default data store.

Dashboard actions, focus, caret and selection use the dashboard neutral palette throughout, including login and payment dialogs. Pro keeps the filled primary upgrade; Business uses an outlined secondary upgrade. Capacity warnings retain red warning semantics. Public-site raspberry remains defined by DESIGN.md.

Use neutral grays for navigation, fields and selected controls. No pink, purple or green decorative theme. Keep status color local to errors, warnings and success feedback. Professional hierarchy comes from readable type, consistent spacing and one primary action per task.
